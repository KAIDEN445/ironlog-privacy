IronLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-13
Last updated: 2026-10-26

What IronLog stores

IronLog stores your workouts, plans, measurements, recovery entries, exercise settings and pictures, preferences, and private usage counters on your device. It has no account, sign-up, analytics, advertising, tracking, or app data server.

Where data stays

On iOS and Android, your saved log is encrypted at rest with a key held in the device secure keystore. The web app uses IndexedDB or local storage in your browser profile without an additional IronLog encryption layer. There is no automatic sync or developer-held backup.

Pictures you choose

IronLog reads only a picture you select from your gallery or files for exercise guidance. A copy is saved with the exercise and included in JSON backups. It does not change your gallery original or use your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Alerts, permissions and updates

Notifications are local rest-timer and workout-reminder alerts; vibration provides on-device feedback. Denying notifications does not disable the in-app timer. Alert timing, sound and lock-screen display depend on permissions and system settings, including battery limits, Focus or Do Not Disturb, and Android force-stop. Delivery is not guaranteed. Over-the-air updates are disabled for this release.

Backups and imports

JSON backups include saved pictures and are not encrypted. Export uses the native share sheet or a browser download only when you choose it. Store files safely; a chosen sharing service may receive them. JSON restore replaces current data after review and confirmation; CSV import adds workouts or measurements. Files are read locally. Native picker and sharing cache copies may be unencrypted; IronLog attempts cleanup after use and at the next launch.

External links and contact

Opening Guide sources or contacting support uses other apps or services and their privacy policies. IronLog does not attach your log. Web app files are downloaded from its host, which receives normal request details such as your IP address.

Children

IronLog is not directed at children. Do not enter personal information about children into the app.

Deletion and contact

Use Delete all saved data in Settings > Data & backup to clear local records, including saved exercise pictures. Uninstalling the native app or clearing browser site data can also remove them.
